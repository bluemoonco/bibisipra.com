For recruiters & hiring leaders

Start with the mandate.

Share the role, location and tax remit to explore the fit. The strongest starting point is US corporate tax compliance, ASC 740 and multinational reporting.

What should a role brief include?

The company or industry, title, reporting line, location, work arrangement, expected start date and core tax responsibilities. For an expat role, include relocation and work authorization requirements.

Does the role include a broader international tax remit?

If your mandate includes US inbound/outbound international tax, Subpart F, GILTI, FDII, Pillar Two / BEPS, cross-border structuring or M&A tax due diligence, include the specific responsibilities and required depth in your brief so relevant experience can be discussed.

These are topics for role-fit discussions, rather than a claim of specialist experience in every area.
