Privacy

Professional inquiries

The inquiry form asks for your name, email address and message. Company and role/location are optional. These details are used to respond to professional inquiries and CV requests.

When the form is activated and you submit it, Web3Forms processes the information to deliver your inquiry to Bibi at bibisipra@gmail.com. Web3Forms may retain submission data under its account settings and privacy policy. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy. The destination inbox is hosted by Gmail.

This website does not store drafts in browser storage. Please do not include tax returns, identity documents or other sensitive records in an initial inquiry.

Hosting and technical information

The hosting provider and form service may process connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect their services. The website does not include advertising pixels or site analytics scripts.

Questions about your information
