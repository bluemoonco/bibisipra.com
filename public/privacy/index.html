Privacy

Professional inquiries

The Direct Contact form asks for your name, email address and message. Phone, company and role/location are optional. These details are used only to respond to professional inquiries and CV requests.

Submissions are delivered by Web3Forms to Bibi's email inbox. Web3Forms may retain submission data under its own privacy policy. Please don't include tax returns, identity documents or other sensitive records in an initial inquiry.

Hosting

This site is a static website served by Cloudflare. The hosting provider and form service may process connection information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect their services.

Website analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand visits and how people use its pages. Google Analytics uses cookies and processes information such as pages visited, device and browser details, and referral sources. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.
