Professional inquiries

The Direct Contact form asks for your name, email address and message. Phone, company and role/location are optional. These details are used only to respond to professional inquiries and CV requests.

This site is a static website served by Cloudflare. The hosting provider and form service may process connection information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect their services. The site has no advertising pixels and does not set cookies.
